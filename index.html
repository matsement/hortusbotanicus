Heritage and future

From 2026 to 2050, the Hortus becomes the Green Heart of Technology.

In 2026, the Hortus joined the Faculty of Architecture and the Built Environment and became a living testbed for design, data and biodiversity.

By 2050, the Hortus is a public platform where climate research, urban ecology, plant science and everyday life meet. The result is a space where visitors can see how technology can support a richer, more resilient future.

The story is no longer only about the past; it is about what the garden becomes in the years ahead: a place for experimentation, public learning and collective imagination.

How to get here

By public transport: a short walk from Delft station and the central tram loop.

By bike: covered parking and direct routes from the city centre and campus.

By car: limited visitor parking near the Schie; use the city ring and walk the last stretch.

Accessibility

The building has step-free access, adapted toilets, hearing support and quiet hours every Tuesday morning.

House rules

Stay on marked paths, respect the living lab spaces and follow staff guidance in wetland and greenhouse areas.

Guided tours and walks

Short tours run daily and longer themed walks are available in spring and summer. Book at the entrance or online.

Coffee-star in the Hortus

Grab coffee, local pastries and seasonal lunches in the public café beside the heritage garden.

School visits and venue rental

School groups, workshops and private events can be booked year-round. Venue rental includes the café, greenhouse and event terrace.
