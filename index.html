A living bridge between nature and innovation

More than 100 years of botanical research, reimagined for the climate century.

The Hortus Botanicus has been a place of experimentation, observation, and public wonder for over a century. Wild plants once moved from field to factory, and now they guide a new era of climate resilience, biotechnology, and eco-innovation.

In 2050, the Hortus is the Green Heart of Technology: a living laboratory where biodiversity and digital systems meet, and where visitors can touch the future of nature.
